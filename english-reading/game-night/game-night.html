Part 1 of 3

Fancy a game?

星期五晚上，Wei 帶著零食到 George 家。

  1. G
    George

    Come in, come in! Emma's already here.

    進來進來！Emma 已經到了。

  2. W
    Wei

    Hi, everyone. Thanks for having me. I brought some snacks.

    嗨，大家好。謝謝你們邀請我。我帶了一些零食。

  3. E
    Emma

    Oh, lovely! So, George, what are we playing tonight?

    喔，太好了！George，我們今晚玩什麼？

  4. G
    George

    I've got loads of board games, but I was thinking Dragon Market. Fancy a game, Wei?

    我有一大堆桌遊，不過我在想玩 Dragon Market。Wei，要不要玩一局？

  5. W
    Wei

    Sure, but I've never played it before.

    好啊，不過我從來沒玩過。

  6. E
    Emma

    Don't worry, it's really easy. You'll pick it up in no time.

    別擔心，很簡單。你很快就會上手。

  7. G
    George

    Right, let's set up. Emma, can you put the board in the middle?

    好，我們來把遊戲擺好。Emma，可以把圖板放中間嗎？

  8. E
    Emma

    Sure. Wei, pick a counter. What colour do you want?

    好。Wei，挑一個棋子。你要什麼顏色？

  9. W
    Wei

    Um, the blue one, I guess.

    嗯……藍色的吧。

  10. G
    George

    Blue for Wei. And I'll shuffle the pack.

    藍色給 Wei。那我來洗牌。

Part 2 of 3

How do you play?

George 講解規則。Wei 第一次聽到 go 這個用法。

  1. W
    Wei

    So, can you explain the rules?

    所以，可以解釋一下規則嗎？

  2. G
    George

    Okay. On your go, you roll the dice and move your counter.

    好。輪到你的時候，你擲骰子，然後移動你的棋子。

  3. W
    Wei

    Sorry, my... go?

    不好意思，我的……go？

  4. E
    Emma

    Your turn. We say “go” as well.

    就是 turn，輪到你。我們也會說 go。

  5. W
    Wei

    Ah, got it.

    喔，懂了。

  6. G
    George

    If you land on a market square, you draw a card from the pack.

    如果你停在市集格，就從牌堆抽一張牌。

  7. E
    Emma

    And you can swap cards with other players, if they agree.

    而且只要對方同意，你可以跟別人換牌。

  8. G
    George

    Every card is worth points. First to ten points wins.

    每張牌都有分數。先拿到十分的人贏。

  9. E
    Emma

    Oh, and watch out for the red squares. If you land on one, you miss a go.

    喔，還有小心紅色的格子。停在上面的話，就要暫停一回合。

  10. W
    Wei

    Right. Roll, move, draw a card... Okay, I think I've got it.

    好。擲骰子、移動、抽牌……好，我想我懂了。

  11. G
    George

    Great. Emma, you deal. Five cards each.

    很好。Emma，你發牌。每人五張。

  12. E
    Emma

    And no peeking at other people's hands!

    還有，不准偷看別人的手牌！

Part 3 of 3

Best of three?

玩到最後，Wei 只差一分。

  1. E
    Emma

    Right, whose go is it?

    好，輪到誰了？

  2. G
    George

    Mine. Hang on... Emma, were you looking at my cards?

    我。等一下……Emma，你剛剛在偷看我的牌嗎？

  3. E
    Emma

    No! You're holding them the wrong way round.

    才沒有！是你把牌拿反了。

  4. G
    George

    Hmm. I'm watching you, you cheat.

    哼。我在盯著你喔，你這個作弊鬼。

  5. W
    Wei

    Okay, my go. I've got nine points. If I draw a gold card, I win.

    好，換我。我有九分。只要抽到金色的牌，我就贏了。

  6. W
    Wei

    Four... market square! And it's... gold! Ten points!

    四……市集格！然後是……金色的！十分！

  7. E
    Emma

    No way! Well played, Wei!

    不會吧！打得好，Wei！

  8. G
    George

    Beginner's luck. That's all it was.

    新手運而已啦。

  9. E
    Emma

    Don't be a sore loser, George. Last week it was a draw, and you complained all night.

    別輸不起嘛，George。上星期是平手，你就抱怨了一整晚。

  10. G
    George

    Fine. Rematch? Best of three?

    好啦。再來一場？三戰兩勝？

  11. W
    Wei

    I'd love to, but it's nearly eleven. Next week?

    我很想，不過快十一點了。下星期？

  12. G
    George

    Deal. Come on, let's tidy it away.

    一言為定。來吧，把遊戲收好。

  13. W
    Wei

    Thanks, guys. That was really fun.

    謝謝大家，今晚真的很好玩。
