到場

At the door

B1 入口，先報到、付錢。

  • Hi, is this the game night?

    嗨，請問這裡是桌遊之夜嗎？

  • I paid in advance. It should be under my name.

    我有先付錢，應該登記在我的名字下面。

    under my name：用我的名字登記的。訂位、報名都這樣說。

  • I didn't RSVP. Can I still pay at the door?

    我沒有事先報名，還可以現場付嗎？

    RSVP 可以當動詞，意思是回覆會不會出席。傳單寫現場付 150。

  • Where can I leave my stuff?

    我的東西可以放哪裡？

    stuff 是隨身東西的口語說法。

  • Mind if I just look around first?

    我先到處看看可以嗎？

認識人

Meeting people

大部分是外國學生，你是在地人。開場、追問、接話。

  • Hey, I don't think we've met. I'm name.

    嗨，我們好像還沒見過。我是……

    比 Nice to meet you 先講，比較自然。

  • Sorry, what was your name again?

    抱歉，你剛剛說你叫什麼？

    again 表示對方講過了，你忘了。很常用，不失禮。

  • Is this your first time at one of these?

    你是第一次來這種活動嗎？

  • So what brought you to Taiwan?

    所以你怎麼會來台灣？

  • Are you here for your whole degree, or just a semester?

    你是整個學位都在這裡唸，還是只待一學期？

    交換生可以直接問：Are you on exchange?

  • What program are you in?

    你唸哪個系所／學程？

    研究生之間常用 program，比 major 自然。

  • How are you liking Taiwan so far?

    目前在台灣還喜歡嗎？

  • Have you been to a night market yet?

    你去過夜市了嗎？

    yet 放句尾，問「到目前為止」。

  • Wait, where in country are you from?

    等等，你是那個國家的哪裡人？

    對方說了國家之後追問，表示你有在聽。

  • If you ever need food recs around here, just ask.

    如果你需要這附近的美食推薦，儘管問我。

    recs 是 recommendations 的口語縮寫。

  • I'm local, so I can help if you need anything.

    我是本地人，有需要可以找我。

  • How do you know everyone here?

    你怎麼認識這裡的人的？

    聊不下去時的萬用救援句。

    你們這局玩到一半嗎？

遊戲

The games on the flyer

Wavelength

心靈同步（猜頻率）Team guessing

How it works

Two teams take turns. One player, the Psychic, secretly sees where the target is and draws a card with two opposite ends, like Hot and Cold. The Psychic gives one clue that sits somewhere between those two ends, and then says nothing more. Their team talks it over and turns the dial to where they think the target is. The other team then guesses whether the target is to the left or right of the dial. The closer the dial is to the center of the target, the more points (2, 3 or 4). A correct left/right guess earns the other team 1 point, unless the dial hit the 4-point center. The first team to 10 points wins.

兩隊輪流。每回合一個人當 Psychic，偷看目標區在哪裡，抽一張兩端相反的卡（例如 Hot–Cold），然後只給一個提示，之後不能再講話。隊友討論、轉指針。另一隊猜目標在指針的左邊還是右邊。指針越靠近目標中心，分數越高（2、3、4 分）；對手猜對左右得 1 分（指針正中 4 分區時除外）。先到 10 分的隊伍贏。

Words you'll hear

Things to say

  • Way more to the right.

    再往右很多。

    這句和下一句都出自官方規則書的例子。

  • Maybe a tiny bit more to the left?

    也許再往左一點點？

  • Wait, hot as in temperature, or hot as in spicy?

    等等，是溫度的熱，還是辣的那種熱？

    as in：「指的是……那個意思」。猜提示時超好用。

  • No talking, Psychic!

    出題的人不能講話！

    規則：給完提示就不能再講話。

  • We're totally on the same wavelength.

    我們完全心有靈犀。

    on the same wavelength：想法一致，遊戲名就是從這來的。

Werewolf

狼人殺Social deduction

How it works

A moderator runs the game and doesn't play. At night everyone closes their eyes. The werewolves silently pick a victim, and the Seer checks whether one player is a werewolf. In the morning the moderator says who died. During the day everyone argues about who the werewolves are, then votes one player out. Dead players can't talk. The villagers win when every werewolf is dead. The werewolves win when there are as many of them as there are villagers.

主持人帶遊戲，自己不玩。晚上大家閉眼，狼人安靜地選一個人殺，預言家查驗一個人是不是狼。早上主持人宣布誰死了。白天大家討論誰是狼，然後投票放逐一個人。死掉的人不能講話。狼全死，村民贏；狼的人數等於村民，狼人贏。

常見的另一個版本是 One Night Ultimate Werewolf：只有一晚一天，有些角色會換牌，所以你最後可能不是一開始的角色；最後大家同時投一次票。開始前先問 Which version are we playing?

Words you'll hear

Things to say

  • Which version are we playing?

    我們玩哪個版本？

  • Who's going to moderate?

    誰要當主持人？

    moderate 當動詞：主持。

  • I'm just a villager, I swear.

    我真的只是村民，我發誓。

  • I'm claiming Seer. I checked her last night, and she's a werewolf.

    我跳預言家。我昨晚查了她，她是狼。

    claim 在這裡是「宣稱自己是某個角色」。

  • That's exactly what a werewolf would say.

    狼人才會這樣說。

  • Why are you being so quiet?

    你怎麼這麼安靜？

    安靜的人常被懷疑，這句會常聽到。

  • Who are you voting for?

    你要投誰？

  • I'm dead, so I can't say anything.

    我死了，所以我不能講話。

UNO

UNOCard game

How it works

Everyone starts with seven cards. On your turn, play a card that matches the top of the discard pile by color, number or symbol. If you can't play, draw one card. If it fits, you can play it right away. When you play your second-to-last card, you have to say “UNO.” If someone catches you before the next player's turn starts, you draw two. A Wild Draw Four is only allowed when you have no card of the current color, and the next player can challenge it. The official rules don't allow stacking a Draw Two on a Draw Two, but plenty of groups play that way, so ask first.

每人七張。輪到你時，出一張跟棄牌堆最上面同色、同數字或同符號的牌。出不了就抽一張，能出可以馬上出。出到剩一張時要喊 UNO，下一個人出牌前被抓到沒喊，罰抽兩張。+4 只能在你手上沒有目前顏色時出，下一家可以質疑。官方規則不能 +2 疊 +2，但很多人會這樣玩，開始前先問。

Words you'll hear

Things to say

    可以疊加嗎？

    問 +2 能不能疊 +2。

  • Any house rules I should know about?

    有什麼自訂規則我要先知道嗎？

    house rules：這群人自己的規則。

  • You didn't say UNO! Draw two.

    你沒喊 UNO！抽兩張。

  • I'll make it blue.

    我指定藍色。

    出萬用牌時說。

  • Wait, which way are we going?

    等等，現在是往哪邊輪？

    有人出 Reverse 之後常問。

  • I'm challenging that Draw Four.

    我要質疑那張 +4。

Mahjong

台灣麻將You're the local

How it works

Taiwanese mahjong is played with 16 tiles in your hand, and you win with 17: five sets plus a pair. There are three suits numbered 1 to 9 (dots, bamboo and characters), plus winds and dragons. Flowers are bonus tiles: set them aside and draw again. On your turn you draw a tile from the wall and discard one. Play goes counter-clockwise. You can take any player's discard for a pung (three of a kind) or a kong (four), but only the player right before you for a chow (a run). You win by drawing the last tile yourself (self-draw), or with someone's discard.

台灣麻將手上 16 張，胡牌時 17 張：五組加一對。三種花色各 1 到 9（筒、條、萬），加上風牌和三元牌；花牌是加分牌，放旁邊再補一張。輪到你摸一張、打一張，逆時針進行。碰、槓可以拿任何人打的牌，吃只能吃上家。自摸或吃別人打的牌都可以胡。

外國朋友如果玩過麻將，可能是香港麻將（13 張、胡牌 14 張）或美式麻將，規則差很多，開始前講清楚是台灣 16 張。英文喊 “Mahjong!” 胡牌，台灣是喊「胡」。一張牌能不能多人同時胡，各地說法不同，當作自訂規則先講好。

Words you'll hear

Things to say

  • Have you played mahjong before? Which version?

    你玩過麻將嗎？哪個版本？

  • Taiwanese mahjong is a bit different. We hold sixteen tiles, not thirteen.

    台灣麻將有點不一樣，我們手上拿十六張，不是十三張。

  • You can only chow from the person on your left.

    吃只能吃你左邊那家（上家）。

    逆時針打，上家就坐你左邊。

  • I'll take that for a pung.

    那張我要碰。

    pung 也拼成 pong。

  • I'm one tile away. I'm ready.

    我差一張，聽牌了。

  • Self-draw! Everyone pays.

    自摸！大家都要付。

    台灣算法：自摸三家付，放槍只有放槍的人付。

  • Ugh, I discarded your winning tile.

    唉，我放槍了。

    放槍沒有固定英文；Wiktionary 收了 deal in，但這樣直接講最清楚。

  • Let's skip the scoring for now and just play.

Explain it in a minute

你很可能要教外國人打。照這段講，一句一句練到順。

  1. This is Taiwanese mahjong. We hold sixteen tiles, not thirteen like in Hong Kong.

    這是台灣麻將。我們拿十六張，不像香港是十三張。

  2. There are three suits, numbered one to nine: dots, bamboo, and characters.

    有三種花色，一到九：筒子、條子、萬子。

  3. Then there are the winds, and three dragons: red, green, and white.

    另外還有風牌，和三種三元牌：中、發、白。

  4. If you draw a flower, put it to the side and draw another tile.

    摸到花牌就放旁邊，再補一張。

  5. On your turn, you draw one tile and discard one.

    輪到你就摸一張、打一張。

  6. To win, you need five sets and a pair.

    要胡牌，你需要五組加一對。

  7. A set is either a run in one suit, like four, five, six, or three of the same tile.

    一組可以是同花色的順子，像四五六，或是三張一樣的。

  8. You can grab anyone's discard to make three of a kind. That's called a pung.

    任何人打的牌都可以拿來湊三張一樣的，這叫碰。

  9. But you can only take a discard for a run from the person on your left. That's a chow.

    但要湊順子，只能拿你左邊那家打的牌，這叫吃。

  10. If you draw the winning tile yourself, everyone pays you. If you win off someone's discard, only that person pays.

    自己摸到胡的牌，大家都付你；吃別人的牌胡，只有打那張的人付。

  11. Don't worry about the scoring yet. Just play, and I'll help you.

    先別管怎麼算分，打就對了，我會幫你。

Flip 7

Flip 7Push your luck

How it works

The dealer goes around asking each player, “Hit or stay?” Hit means you take another card. Stay means you stop and bank your points for the round. If you get a number you already have, you bust and score nothing that round. If you collect seven different numbers, the round ends right away and you get a 15-point bonus. Action cards change things: Freeze makes a player bank and stop, Flip Three makes a player take three cards, and Second Chance saves you from one bust. The game ends when someone has 200 points or more at the end of a round.

發牌的人一個個問：Hit or stay? Hit 是再拿一張，stay 是停下來，把這回合的分數存起來。拿到重複的數字就爆牌，這回合零分。集滿七個不同數字，回合立刻結束，加 15 分。功能牌：Freeze 讓人停下並入袋，Flip Three 逼人連拿三張，Second Chance 可以抵一次爆牌。回合結束時有人到 200 分以上，遊戲結束。

    跟 21 點一樣的說法。

  • I'm giving you Flip Three.

  • Should I push my luck?

    push your luck：冒險繼續拿。

Moose Master

Moose MasterParty rules game

How it works

At the start, pick two Moose Rules that everyone has to follow, like “You can't say the word no.” On your turn, flip a card and do what it says. Some cards are quick actions, like pointing left or right, and the last person to do it takes a penalty card. Other cards give you a power for a while, like Thumb Master: when you put your thumb down, the last person to copy you takes a penalty. If you catch someone breaking a rule, call it out, and they take a penalty card. Depending on how you play, you're either out after seven penalty cards, or the player with the fewest penalties at the end wins.

開始時選兩條所有人都要遵守的 Moose Rules，例如「不能說 No」。輪到你就翻一張牌照做。有些是快速動作，例如指左、指右，最後一個做的人拿懲罰牌。有些讓你暫時拿到特權，例如 Thumb Master：你大拇指放下時，最後一個跟著放的人要拿懲罰牌。抓到有人犯規就當場說出來，對方拿懲罰牌。玩法二選一：拿滿七張懲罰牌出局，或打完整副牌、懲罰牌最少的人贏。

Words you'll hear

Things to say

  • What are the Moose Rules this round?

    這局的禁令是什麼？

  • You said “no”! Take a penalty card.

    你說了 no！拿一張懲罰牌。

  • Gotcha!

    抓到了！

  • Wait, did I just break a rule?

    等等，我剛剛犯規了嗎？

  • Who's the Thumb Master right now?

    現在誰是拇指王？

Pool (8-ball)

撞球Table game

How it works

There's a white cue ball and fifteen numbered balls: solids are 1 to 7, stripes are 9 to 15, and the 8 is black. After the break, the table is open. The first ball you legally pocket decides whether you're solids or stripes. Once all your balls are down, you call a pocket and sink the 8 to win. You lose if you sink the 8 too early, sink it in a pocket you didn't call, or sink it on a foul. If you scratch (pocket the cue ball) or hit the wrong ball first, it's a foul, and in standard rules your opponent gets ball in hand. Bar rules vary a lot from place to place, so agree on them before you start.

一顆白色母球，十五顆子球：1 到 7 是小花（全色），9 到 15 是大花（條紋），8 號是黑球。開球後是開放球局，第一顆合法進袋的球決定你打小花還是大花。自己的球全部打完後，指定袋口把 8 號打進就贏。8 號太早進、進錯指定袋口、或犯規時進，都直接輸。母球洗袋、先碰到錯的球都是犯規，標準規則下對方拿自由球。酒吧規則每個地方不同，先講好。

注意：在台灣「桌球」是指乒乓球，撞球的英文是 pool 或 billiards。

    要打一局 8 號球嗎？

  • I'll rack. You break.

    我來排球，你開球。

  • So I'm solids, and you're stripes.

    所以我打小花，你打大花。

  • Eight ball, corner pocket.

    8 號球，角落袋口。

    打 8 號前要先指定袋口。

  • Oh no, I scratched.

    糟了，母球洗袋了。

  • Are we playing ball in hand?

    犯規要給自由球嗎？

    酒吧規則常沒有自由球，先問。

  • Nice shot!

    好球！

  • Can you pass me the chalk?

    可以把巧克遞給我嗎？

Ping-pong

桌球（乒乓球）Table game

How it works

A game goes to 11, and you have to win by 2. The serve switches every two points. At 10–10, it switches every point. If a serve touches the net but is otherwise good, it's a let, and you serve again. During a rally, a ball that clips the net and goes over is still in. A ball that hits the top edge of the table counts. One that hits the side doesn't. In doubles, partners have to take turns hitting the ball.

一局打到 11 分，要贏 2 分以上。每兩分換發球，10 平之後每一分換。發球擦網但其他都合法，叫 let，重發；來回中擦網過去照樣算。打到桌子上緣的擦邊球算，打到側面不算。雙打時兩個隊友要輪流擊球。

英文 ping-pong 和 table tennis 都可以。球拍美式說 paddle，英式常說 bat，正式規則用 racket。

  • That's a let. Serve again.

    那是擦網，重發。

Jackbox: Quiplash

Jackbox 派對遊戲Phones + one screen

How it works

The game runs on one shared screen, and everyone plays on their own phone. Go to jackbox.tv in your phone's browser and type in the room code from the screen. The first person to join is the VIP and starts the game. Quiplash takes three to eight players; anyone after that joins as the audience and can still vote. You get a prompt and type the funniest answer you can. Two players answer each prompt, and everyone else votes for the one they like more. If every player votes for your answer, that's a Quiplash, and you get bonus points. The final round is different in each version, so just follow the screen.

大螢幕放遊戲，每個人用自己的手機玩。手機瀏覽器打開 jackbox.tv，輸入螢幕上的 room code。第一個加入的人是 VIP，負責按開始。Quiplash 可以三到八人玩，之後加入的人變成觀眾，一樣可以投票。每題給你一個題目（prompt），你打一個最好笑的答案。每題兩個人回答，其他人投票選喜歡的。所有玩家都投給你，就是 Quiplash，有加分。最後一回合每個版本不一樣，跟著螢幕走就好。
