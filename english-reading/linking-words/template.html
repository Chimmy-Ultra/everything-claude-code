Everyday Linking Words

Linking words tie your ideas together: but, because, although, however… This page has {{N_WORDS}} of the most useful ones, {{N_EX}} example sentences you can listen to, and {{N_TRAPS}} common traps. Start with the map below. It explains why but and however are used differently.

Four ways to link ideas

Every linking word on this page works in one of these ways. The coloured label on each card tells you which.

joins把兩個句子連成一句
It was raining,butwe went out.

Also: so · because · although · when · if · unless · until

starts開始新的一句，後面加逗號
It was raining.However,we went out.

Also: Besides, · On top of that, · That's why · Otherwise, · For example,

+ noun後面接名詞，不接句子
Because ofthe rain,we stayed in.

Also: like · such as · after dinner · until nine

at the end放在句子最後
It was cold.It was sunny,though.

Also: too · as well
