Meet the Neighbor

這頁用來試聽新的聲音。音檔是用開源語音模型 Kokoro 預先錄好的，不再用你電腦內建的語音，所以換電腦、換瀏覽器聽起來都一樣。點任何一句就會播那一句。

Saying hi next door

    Karen

    Hi there! You must be the new neighbor. I'm Karen, from 4B.

    嗨！你一定就是新鄰居吧。我是 Karen，住 4B。

  2. W
    Wei

    Hi, Karen. I'm Wei. I just moved in last week.

    嗨，Karen。我是 Wei，上星期剛搬進來。

  3. K
    Karen

    Oh, welcome! How are you settling in?

    喔，歡迎！安頓得還好嗎？

  4. W
    Wei

    Pretty well, thanks. I'm still unpacking, though. There are boxes everywhere.

    還不錯，謝謝。不過我還在拆箱，到處都是箱子。

  5. K
    Karen

    Ha, I remember that feeling. If you need anything, just let me know.

    哈，我記得那種感覺。需要什麼的話，跟我說一聲就好。

  6. W
    Wei

    That's really kind of you. Actually, do you know when they pick up the garbage?

    你人真好。對了，你知道垃圾什麼時候收嗎？

  7. K
    Karen

    Tuesday mornings. Just put it out the night before.

    星期二早上。前一天晚上拿出去放就好。

  8. W
    Wei

    Got it. Thanks, that's really helpful.

    了解，謝謝，這很有幫助。

    Karen

    No problem. Anyway, I'll let you get back to your boxes. See you around!

Compare the voices

同一句話，六種聲音。之後的對話可以挑你喜歡的聲音來錄。

“Hi, I just moved in next door. It's really nice to meet you.”

上面的對話裡，Karen 用 Heart，Wei 用 Michael。Emma 和 George 是英國口音。

聲音：開源語音模型 Kokoro（v1.0）預先產生的音檔，每句一個檔案。對話是為了學習而寫的，不是真實錄音。

各國用字參考：Collins Dictionary: garbage、Speakspeak: rubbish, trash, garbage。
